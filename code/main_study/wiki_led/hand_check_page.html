How to mark links
  • Each card pairs a Seattle library book with the Wikipedia article that a matching rule linked to it, either the book's own article or its author's.
  • Mark Right if the article is about that book (or that author), Wrong if it is about something or someone else, Not sure if you cannot tell. Open the article when the description is not enough.
  • Keys: 1 right, 2 wrong, 3 not sure, ← → move. Each answer is saved as you go.
How to choose themes
  • Each card is a Wikipedia page that many people read. Choose the themes whose library books a reader interested in this topic would naturally look for: up to 3, strongest first, or fits no theme.
  • Use the text shown and what you know; please don't look the page up. Search by any word (a theme, its group, or a subject heading), or browse the groups.
  • Keys: Enter in the search adds the top result, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter saves and moves on. Each card is saved when you press Save.
