# Edmonton Public Library top-10 holds: profile (15 Oct 2026)

Source: City of Edmonton Open Data, dataset qdgm-hex6, City of Edmonton Open Data Terms of Use v2.1. File data/raw/edmonton_holds/edmonton_top10_holds.csv, 14.4 MB, downloaded 15 Oct 2026 02:38.

- 47,764 rows: for each of 22 branches, the 10 titles with the most holds, in weekly snapshots.
- 235 snapshots from 2 February 2015 to 18 October 2021, with gaps: one snapshot in 2017 Q1, almost none from 2018 Q4 to 2019 Q4, none in 2020 Q3.
- 5,585 distinct titles (title and author as text, no subject headings, no format column).
- During the 2020 closures the snapshots repeat the same numbers week after week (for example one title held at 27 holds at one branch from 4 May to 22 June 2020), so holds were frozen and cannot show the June 2020 rise.

Use: not usable for the theme analysis (only the ten most-held titles per branch, no subjects, frozen in 2020). It could support a later single-title paper on queues for new releases, after matching titles to Seattle works. Nothing in the thesis uses it.
